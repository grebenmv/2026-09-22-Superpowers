# Ideas

This folder contains new ideas for the wine bar and Georgian restaurant.

Examples include:

- content ideas for Instagram and Telegram;
- promotion and campaign concepts;
- ideas for events, collaborations, and partnerships;
- ideas for the website, menus, and wine card;
- ideas for improving online presence and customer engagement;
- possible automation ideas.

Ideas do not need to be approved or fully developed yet. Each idea should explain the concept, the intended business goal, and any useful context or source.
