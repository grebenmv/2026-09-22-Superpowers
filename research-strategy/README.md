# Research Strategy

This folder contains plans and materials for researching the market, audience, competitors, communication channels, and promotion opportunities.

Examples include:

- research questions and objectives;
- target audience research;
- competitor and local market reviews;
- analysis of Instagram, Telegram, websites, maps, and aggregators;
- customer feedback and review analysis;
- plans for collecting and comparing data;
- research findings and conclusions;
- sources, links, and dates of research.

Each research document should state what is being investigated, why it matters, what information is needed, and what decision the research may support.
